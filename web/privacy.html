Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This notice explains, in plain words, what happens to what you type into this demo.

1. Who is responsible

This demo is a personal portfolio project run by Kevin Pino Arias, an individual, in Glasgow, United Kingdom. The owner decides why and how your question is processed (the "controller" in data-protection terms). For anything in this notice, write to kevin.pino.arias@outlook.com.

2. What the service does with your question

The demo is shown to a few invited people, and asking a question needs an access code, which comes in the link you were sent. You type a question. The service reads it, searches public legal texts, and, when its language model is available, sends your question and the passages it found to Google's Gemini service to decide how to handle the question and to write the answer. If that service is unavailable, the demo answers without it (it shows passages and exact figures only).

3. What is kept

One line is written for each question.
KeptNot kept
Its language; which route the demo took; the identifiers of the passages it found; how long each step took; how it ended (answer, exact figure, refusal, "not enough evidence", error). The text of your question; your network (IP) address; your name or any account (there are none); cookies (none are set); analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

To limit how many questions can be asked, the server counts the questions it receives in total. It never reads or stores your network address. Connecting to any website shows your address to the company that runs the server (Microsoft, through its Azure Container Apps service); that company may keep its own technical logs under its own policy, and this project does not read them.

Your browser stores your language choice (English or Spanish) in its local storage so the page remembers it, and keeps the access code for the current browser tab only. Neither leaves your device except that the code is sent with each question.

4. Why, and on what legal basis

To answer your question, to keep the service secure and affordable (limits on how often it can be used), and to measure its speed and reliability. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and securing a free demo (Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR, and of the EU GDPR for visitors in the European Union). You may object (see section 8).

5. How long

The lines described in section 3 are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.

6. Who receives it

  • Google (Gemini API) processes your question and the retrieved passages to produce the answer, as a service provider acting on our instructions under the terms for its paid services.
  • The hosting provider (Microsoft Azure) runs the server.
  • Nobody else. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Their own terms set out the safeguards that apply.

7. Please do not type personal data

Do not put names, identity numbers, health details or other personal data into the question box: although this service does not keep the text of your question, it is sent to Google to produce the answer. The demo explains what public legal texts say. It is not legal advice, it can be wrong, and it declines to judge your own situation.

8. Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict its use, or object to it. Because this service keeps no question text, no account and no address, there is normally nothing of yours for us to find; you may still write to the contact address above. You may also complain to a data-protection authority: in the United Kingdom, where this demo is run, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es); or the authority of your own country.

9. Automated decisions, children, changes

The demo makes no decision about you with legal or similar effect. It is not aimed at children. If this notice changes, the date at the top changes.
