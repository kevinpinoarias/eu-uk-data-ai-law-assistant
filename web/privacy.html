Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This notice explains, in plain words, what happens to what you type into this demo.

1. Who is responsible

This demo is a personal portfolio project run by [OWNER NAME], an individual, in [CITY, COUNTRY]. For anything in this notice, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What the service does with your question

You type a question. The service reads it, searches public legal texts, and, when its language model is available, sends your question and the passages it found to Google's Gemini service to decide how to handle the question and to write the answer. If that service is unavailable, the demo answers without it (it shows passages and exact figures only).

3. What is kept

One line is written for each question.
KeptNot kept
The text of your question; its language; which route the demo took; the identifiers of the passages it found; how long each step took; how it ended (answer, exact figure, refusal, "not enough evidence", error). Your network (IP) address; your name or any account (there are none); cookies (none are set); analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

To limit how many questions one visitor can ask a minute, the server holds a technical identifier of your connection (your network address) in its memory only, for at most 24 hours, and never writes it to the log. The company that hosts the server ([HOSTING PROVIDER]) may keep its own technical logs under its own policy.

Your browser stores your language choice (English or Spanish) in its local storage so the page remembers it. That never leaves your device.

4. Why, and on what legal basis

To answer your question, to keep the service secure and affordable (limits on how often it can be used), and to find and fix errors and measure speed and quality. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving a free demo (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). You may object (see section 8).

5. How long

Questions are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.

6. Who receives it

  • Google (Gemini API) processes your question and the retrieved passages to produce the answer, as a service provider acting on our instructions under the terms for its paid services.
  • The hosting provider runs the server.
  • Nobody else. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Their own terms set out the safeguards that apply.

7. Please do not type personal data

Do not put names, identity numbers, health details or other personal data into the question box. The demo explains what public legal texts say. It is not legal advice, it can be wrong, and it declines to judge your own situation.

8. Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your question, ask us to restrict its use, or object to it. Because the log has no account and no address, we can only find your question if you tell us its exact wording and roughly when you asked it; send that to the contact address above. You may also complain to a data-protection authority: in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es); in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office; or the authority of your own country.

9. Automated decisions, children, changes

The demo makes no decision about you with legal or similar effect. It is not aimed at children. If this notice changes, the date at the top changes.

1. Quién es el responsable

2. Qué hace el servicio con tu pregunta

Escribes una pregunta. El servicio la lee, busca en textos legales públicos y, cuando su modelo de lenguaje está disponible, envía tu pregunta y los pasajes encontrados al servicio Gemini de Google para decidir cómo tratarla y redactar la respuesta. Se conservaNo se conserva

Para responder a tu pregunta, para mantener el servicio seguro y asequible (límites de uso) y para encontrar y corregir errores y medir su velocidad y calidad. La base jurídica es nuestro interés legítimo en gestionar, proteger y mejorar una demostración gratuita (artículo 6.1.f del RGPD). Puedes oponerte (véase el apartado 8).

Las preguntas se conservan 30 días y después se eliminan automáticamente.

  • El proveedor de alojamiento ejecuta el servidor.

Estos proveedores pueden tratar datos fuera del Espacio Económico Europeo y del Reino Unido. Sus propias condiciones establecen las garantías aplicables.

No pongas nombres, números de identificación, datos de salud ni otros datos personales en el cuadro de pregunta. La demostración explica lo que dicen textos legales públicos. No es asesoramiento jurídico, puede equivocarse y se niega a valorar tu situación concreta.

Puedes pedir el acceso, la rectificación o la supresión de tu pregunta, solicitar la limitación de su uso u oponerte a él. Como el registro no tiene cuenta ni dirección, solo podemos encontrar tu pregunta si nos indicas su redacción exacta y más o menos cuándo la hiciste; envíalo a la dirección de contacto indicada. También puedes reclamar ante una autoridad de protección de datos: en España, la Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es); en el Reino Unido, la Information Commissioner's Office; o la autoridad de tu país.

9. Decisiones automatizadas, menores y cambios

La demostración no toma ninguna decisión sobre ti con efectos jurídicos o similares. No está dirigida a menores. Si este aviso cambia, cambiará la fecha de arriba.
